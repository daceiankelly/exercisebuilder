# Exercise profile (CANNED MOCK ANSWER - not written by the API)

## 1. Exercise type and overall shape
Functional exercise, 4 hours, real time with no time jumps. The EOC and agency liaison officers play. Exercise Control (EXCON) simulates field teams, media, public and all outside agencies.

## 2. Organisation and roles
Lead: Marlow Valley EOC (Controller, Operations, Planning, Logistics, PIO). Partners: Fire and Rescue, Police, Regional Ambulance Service, Council Public Works, Community Welfare Team, Valley Power.

## 3. Objectives
Six objectives numbered O1 to O6, each one sentence starting with a verb, some with a measurable target.

## 4. Phases and pacing
Four phases: warning and activation, escalation, peak impact, transition to recovery. One inject every 20 minutes. One unexpected complication late in phase 3.

## 5. Inject format
Number, time, from, to, method, message (two or three plain sentences with specific figures), three expected actions, objective reference.

## 6. Conventions
Messages start and end with EXERCISE EXERCISE EXERCISE. NO DUFF stops play for a real emergency. 24-hour times, metric units.

## 7. Deck structure
Title, administration and safety, aim and objectives, agencies table, scenario background, timeline table, one slide per inject with expected actions in speaker notes, ENDEX and hot debrief.

## 8. Evaluation and debrief
Evaluators time each expected action and rate objectives Met, Partly met or Not met. Four hot debrief questions.

## 9. Lessons to test again
Evacuation decision speed, one situation report, rumour handling by the PIO, backup communications, vulnerable facilities register, out-of-hours start.
